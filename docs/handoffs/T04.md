## Known limitations / notes for Lead

- Tracker operates in 2D image coordinates (raw/reference px).
- Association uses Hungarian matching (`scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment`) with cost-gated rejection.
- Does not depend on ground truth or external libraries outside `numpy` and `scipy`.
