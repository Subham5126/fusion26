## 1. Executive Summary & Experimental Objectives

The goal of Task T15 is to evaluate how the OrbitTrace tracking system performs under difficult observational conditions and quantify which temporal mechanisms genuinely improve tracking reliability.

### Key Hypotheses Tested
1. **Temporal Confirmation Filter (Ablation C):** Single-frame candidate detections suffer from high false-alarm rates in astronomical imagery (hot pixels, transient sensor noise, and background clutter). Enforcing a temporal confirmation threshold of $N \ge 3$ consecutive/associated observations will eliminate spurious clutter while retaining genuine orbital candidates.
2. **Missed-Frame Continuity (Ablation B):** Tolerating up to $M = 2$ consecutive missed detections via motion-aware Kalman/linear state projection will prevent track fragmentation and identity switches during transient object dropouts (e.g. sensor readout fading, short occlusions).
3. **Association Gating Sensitivity (Ablation D):** A 20.0 px gating distance provides the optimal trade-off between accommodating fast apparent motion (up to 19 px/frame) and rejecting false assignments. Overly tight gates (<10 px) induce severe track fragmentation, while excessively wide gates (>30 px) risk false associations.
4. **Trajectory Extrapolation (Ablation E):** Constant-velocity least-squares trajectory extrapolation ($x(t) = x_0 + v_x \Delta t$) dramatically reduces forward-prediction localization error relative to a static zero-order hold baseline.

## 2. Experimental Methodology & Provenance

### Strict Protocol Safeguards (Per `docs/EVALUATION.md`)
- **Zero Leakage:** Sequence-level disjoint partitioning into Development (60%), Validation (20%), and Test (20%) splits using deterministic SHA-256 seed hashing (`create_sequence_splits()`).
- **Evaluator-Only Ground Truth:** Ground truth positions are strictly isolated from the tracking and trajectory inference modules.
- **Fair Comparisons:** All ablation variants are evaluated over identical candidate detections and identical frame sequences.
- **Micro-Aggregated Metrics:** Evaluated counts (TP, FP, FN) are aggregated over the entire benchmark before computing micro precision, recall, and F1.
- **Safe Zero-Denominator Handling:** When denominators evaluate to zero, metrics are reported as `null` accompanied by structured reason codes (`null_reasons`). Undefined precision on empty negative scenes is never reported as 1.0.

### Dataset & Hardware Provenance
- **Synthetic Benchmark Suite:** 30 randomized 5-frame sequences (Seed: `20261009`, matching gate: 5.0 px). Total raw proposals: 408; Total ground-truth points: 115.
- **Execution Environment:** Windows, Python 3.13.5, SciPy 1.15.2 (`linear_sum_assignment`), NumPy 2.2.4.

### Key Ablation Insights
1. **Missed-Detection Handling (Ablation B):** Disabling dropout tolerance (`max_consecutive_misses=0`) drops recall from 93.9% to 85.2% and causes 7 identity switches because a single missed observation fragments the track. Setting `max_consecutive_misses=2` maintains complete continuity without fragmentation.
2. **Confirmation Threshold Invariant (Ablation C):** In Contract 0.1.0, `confirmation_observations < 3` is explicitly disallowed by schema validation (`Track.status == 'confirmed'` requires $\ge 3$ hits). Increasing the threshold to 5 observations severely penalizes recall (drops to 69.6%) whenever targets enter late or experience dropouts. Threshold $N=3$ is optimal.
3. **Association Gating (Ablation D):** A tight 5.0 px gate is fatal for moving objects with velocity $>5\text{ px/frame}$, yielding 46 identity switches and cutting recall in half (43.5%). Conversely, widening the gate to 40 px introduces false associations with background clutter (3 false positives and 1 false confirmed track). The 20.0 px default is robust.

### Explicit Metric Interpretations for Hard Cases 6 & 7:
- **Case 6 (Proximate Clutter & Association Competition):** Spurious detections placed 8–15 px from the true target fall inside the 20 px gating radius. In frame 0, `c6_clutter_0` is slightly closer to `c6_true_1` than `c6_true_0`, causing Hungarian matching to assign the true target sequence across two confirmed tracks (`track-0001` and `track-0002`). This demonstrates why temporal tracking alone cannot fully disentangle persistent proximate clutter without photometric or profile weighting (1 ID switch, Run B precision 0.50).
- **Case 7 (Dense Clutter Rejection vs. Chance Alignment):** In an empty scene with 125 random uniform false detections across 5 frames ($25\text{ noise points/frame}$), tracking rejects **122 out of 125 spurious detections** (97.6% clutter suppression). Exactly 1 false confirmed track emerged by chance ($3$ random points falling within a 20 px radius across frames 1, 3, 4), yielding $\text{FP}=3$, $\text{TP}=0$, Precision 0.00, and Recall `null` (since $\text{TP}+\text{FN}=0$). Truthfully reporting this chance alignment demonstrates the necessity of statistical significance testing for dense starfields.

## 6. Real-Data Evaluation & Scientific Limitations

### Real ESA SpotGEOv2 Evaluation (`test/1`)
- **Run A (Raw OpenCV/Optimized Detector):** 24 candidate proposals, 5 TP, 19 FP, Recall: 50.0%, Precision: 20.8%.
- **Run B (Raw-Coordinate Tracker):** 0 TP, 10 FP.
- **Scientific Disclosure & Cause:** Uncalibrated camera motion (sidereal drift of 18–28 px/frame) sweeps background stars into apparent motion streaks while the faint satellite moves independently. Running Hungarian association in raw pixel coordinates tracks apparent star streaks rather than the satellite.
- **Deferred Dependency:** Image-plane tracking on real ESA sequences scientifically requires Member 2's **Task T13 (Star-Field Registration & Frame Alignment)** before celestial reference frame tracking can be validated.
